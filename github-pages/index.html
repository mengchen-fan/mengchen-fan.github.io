Mengchen Fan

Ph.D. Candidate · University of Alabama at Birmingham

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Alabama at Birmingham. My research focuses on heterogeneous distributed learning, data fusion, and federated and decentralized systems.

I develop interpretable and reliable learning methods for images, graphs, and medical data, with an emphasis on efficiency, stability, and generalization across clients.
